# StableChocobo
## What does it do?
Have you ever wanted to stable your chocobo and got frustrated because it just pops up an error telling you that you need to unsummon your chocobo before you can stable it? Well, with this plugin, you can fix that issue! 

## How does it work?
As soon as you open a Chocobo Stable, a window will pop up telling you that your chocobo is stabled, and will offer to unsummon it for you with the click of a button, instead of needing to open the companion window. You can then stable your chocobo right after!
